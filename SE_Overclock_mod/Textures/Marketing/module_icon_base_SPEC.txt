module_icon_base_master.png — compositing guide
==============================================

File: module_icon_base_master.png
Size: 1536 x 1024 (wide canvas; crop or pad to a square before DDS export if your pipeline needs 1:1).

Purpose
-------
Single neutral “upgrade chip” master. Same silhouette for every role and level.
You only change:
  (A) hue / tint / overlay on the LEFT vertical band  → role color
  (B) which of the TEN bottom pill segments look “lit” → level 1–10
  (C) optional text on the small top-left label pad   → e.g. ATK, LV07
  (D) optional art inside the CENTER recessed slot    → inner detail

Suggested role tints (RGB hints; tune in editor)
-----------------------------------------------
defense   ~ (100, 210, 255)
attack    ~ (240,  85,  95)
power     ~ (255, 215,  75)
berserker ~ (175, 115, 255)
speed     ~ ( 85, 220, 130)
fortress  ~ (255, 145,  55)

Workflow (image editor or Pillow)
---------------------------------
1. Open the master PNG.
2. Role: isolate the left gray accent column (inside the chip, left of the center slot). Apply Color / Hue-Saturation, or a Multiply layer filled with the role color at moderate opacity, until the strip reads clearly as that role.
3. Level: the bottom row has ten equal pill segments with gaps. For level L (1–10), add glow or inner fill on segments 1 … L (left to right); leave segments L+1 … 10 dark/unlit.
4. Label pad: small rounded plate top-left inside the chip — add monospace or bold UI text if desired.
5. Export: crop tightly to the chip if you need a square icon; resize to 128 or 256; convert to BC7/DXT5 DDS for Space Engineers (texconv, etc.).

Notes
------
- The master intentionally has NO text and NO strong hue on the strip so tinting stays predictable.
- Final in-game icons still live under Textures/GUI/Icons/component/modules/ as .dds until you replace them with exports from this master.
